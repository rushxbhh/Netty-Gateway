Two-Stage Acknowledgement Protocol
Stage 1 — Ingress Accept
ACCEPT|stage=INGRESS|transactionId=…|inboundSequence=…
Emitted immediately after FlatBuffer validation and inbound SPSC ring write. Does not imply sequencing or database persistence.
Stage 2 — Durable Audit ACK
ACK|stage=AUDITED|status=PERSISTED|journalSequence=…
Emitted only after the C++ sequencer assigns a monotonic ID, the Audit Service verifies CRC32C, and PostgreSQL commits the transaction.
